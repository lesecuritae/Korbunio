Korbuino compares regional supermarket offers using a German postal code. Search, filter and sort offers by retailer, and add products to a local shopping list.

The app fetches public retailer data directly and does not require a Korbuino server. Saved offers and shopping lists remain available offline. Refreshing offers requires internet access; some retailers may require manual confirmation or may be temporarily unavailable.

Features:
• Manual postal code entry or optional location permission
• Optional scheduled background refresh
• Local backup export and import
• Optional connection to a self-hosted Korbuino server and KitchenOwl
• Light and dark themes

The F-Droid edition uses OkHttp and Conscrypt without Google Play Services. App updates are handled by F-Droid. Crash reports stay on the device and are not submitted automatically.

Korbuino is an independent project and is not affiliated with the listed retailers. Verify prices and availability with the retailer.
